Top setups

Regime router

  • ADX ≥ 25 with price and EMA 50 above EMA 200 → Uptrend, trend playbook
  • ADX ≤ 20 → Range, reversion playbook
  • Between, or below EMA 200 → stand aside (reversion only above EMA 200)
  • BTC daily below EMA 200 flags trend longs as risk-off

Trend following

  • Breakout: close above prior 20-bar high, EMA 20 > 50 > 200, ADX rising, volume ≥ 1.5× average
  • Pullback: dip to EMA 20 that closes back above it, RSI 14 between 40 and 60
  • Stop 2× ATR; trail 3× ATR from the high (chandelier); exit on close below EMA 50

Mean reversion

  • Price above EMA 200, ADX below 25
  • Z-score(20) ≤ −2 or close below the lower band, and RSI 2 ≤ 10
  • Stop 1.5× ATR; target is the 20-bar mean; exit on RSI 2 > 70 or after 10 bars
